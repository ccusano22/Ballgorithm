    Which source to trust
    
    
    
    
    Every graded game scores each source twice: how far its margin missed the final margin, and how far its total missed the final combined score. Each team keeps both records all season, every game counted equally, and once a team has 10 graded games its margin blend and its total blend each lean toward whichever sources read that team best.

    How a team's weights are set
    
        
      
          
    1. Under 10 graded games: the team borrows its conference's record for each source (once 30 team-games are graded there), then Division I's.
      2. 
          
    2. 10 or more: the team's own average miss per source, pulled toward that borrowed record as if it had 10 extra games. More games, less pull.
      3. 
          
    3. Weight goes in proportion to 1 ÷ miss², so a source that misses by half as much earns four times the say.
      4. 
          
    4. Caps: no source above 45% or below 10%. A game uses the average of both teams' weights, rescaled across the sources that posted a number.
      5. 
          
    5. Reported: any move of 5 points or more shows up in the log below on the day it happens.
